FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can LiveHandle download social media videos?

Yes, for public Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook links when the provider returns a downloadable file. Private, restricted, or deleted posts aren't supported.

Can I download Instagram reels?

Public reels and video posts can be saved when the provider returns the media URL. Private or restricted reels can't.

Which public links can be processed?

Public Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook video or reel links the resolver can reach.

Can I download private videos?

No. Private, geo-blocked, deleted, or restricted posts aren't supported.

Does LiveHandle host the original media permanently?

No. Streams are proxied, and temporary processing files may exist briefly for photo slideshows.

What if a link cannot be resolved?

Check that the URL is public and complete, then retry. Provider blocks, deletions, or unsupported formats return a clear error.
