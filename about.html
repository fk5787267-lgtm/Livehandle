Overview

What LiveHandle is

LiveHandle is a free web toolkit for exploring publicly available social-media profile signals. It helps people review public Instagram and TikTok account cues, recent viewership samples when providers return them, simple engagement math, content-idea heuristics, and public video link resolution.

LiveHandle is not affiliated with Instagram, TikTok, Meta, ByteDance, or any other platform named on this site.

Purpose

Why LiveHandle exists

  • No invented numbers. Missing likes, comments, or play counts show as N/A, never as zero.
  • Signals, not verdicts. Authenticity scores summarize public risk signals. They do not prove who runs an account.
  • No passwords. You never sign in to a platform to use these tools.
Tools available

One place for practical creator tools

Use the profile analyzer for public account signals, the TikTok growth checker for a current follower snapshot and recent-view sample, the video downloader for eligible public links, and Trends for content brainstorming.

Visit Learn for guidance on interpreting public metrics and their limits.

Transparency

Our transparency commitment

Platform access can change, and public fields may be missing or delayed. We explain when a number is a sample, estimate, or unavailable, and do not present heuristic ideas as live search-volume data.

Privacy

Privacy

Lookups use public data only. Lookup results may be cached briefly on the server to reduce provider load. We do not ask for platform passwords.

Terms

Terms of use

Use the tools only for public content and only where you have the right to do so. Download content only if you own it or are authorized to use it. Results are not a substitute for official platform insights.
